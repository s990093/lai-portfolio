Overview

I participated in a six-week research exchange at the Advanced Measurement Technology Research Center of Fukuoka Institute of Technology. The program focused on two critical applications of deep learning: Tsunami Recognition and Fish Recognition.

This intensive program involved end-to-end model development—from dataset preprocessing and augmentation to training and evaluation. I experimented with various architectures including ResNet and 1D/2D CNN pipelines, specifically tailored for environmental signals and underwater imagery.

Technical Achievements

Model Architecture

Implemented and fine-tuned ResNet architectures and custom 1D/2D CNN pipelines. Optimized models for specific tasks like detecting tsunami signals from sensor data and identifying fish species in underwater footage.

Data Engineering

Developed robust preprocessing pipelines including advanced data augmentation techniques to handle environmental noise and improve model generalization for real-world scenarios.

Professional Growth

International Collaboration

"Throughout the program, I communicated exclusively in English, enhancing my ability to present technical concepts and collaborate in international research environments. I actively reviewed literature and discussed findings with FIT researchers, strengthening my theoretical foundation."
