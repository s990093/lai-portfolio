03. Immersive Learning

Organizations & Culture

Nike HQ Visit

Explored the Philip H. Knight Campus (400 acres, 75+ buildings). Connected with a Taiwanese engineer who shared insights on:

  • The importance of "soft skills" and self-packaging in the US workplace.
  • Career planning: Stepping out of the comfort zone to increase possibilities.
  • Future trends: Moving from network performance to AI model acceleration.

Nike World Headquarters: A symbol of global influence and innovation.

The campus reflects Nike's commitment to innovation and athlete-centric design.

Rose City Robotics

Met with founder Duncan Miller. They use Transformer networks and imitation learning to train robots.

  • Open-source hardware to lower costs and promote STEM equity.
  • Resilience in entrepreneurship: Maintaining passion despite personal challenges.

Discussing AI robotics education with founder Duncan Miller.

Exploring how open-source hardware and AI can democratize robotics education.
