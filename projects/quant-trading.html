01. Research Background

Origin & Motivation

Origin

This project originated from a course on Programmatic Trading and Quantitative Investment. It represents an effort to extend classroom learning into a robust, real-world trading system by integrating advanced algorithmic optimization and machine learning.

Core Problem

Traditional technical analysis often relies on fixed parameters (e.g., RSI 14) and static rules, which fail to adapt to changing market conditions. This leads to:

  • Inconsistent performance across different periods.
  • High drawdown during market volatility.
  • Difficulty in selecting the "best" indicators.

Our Solution

  • Why PSO?To dynamically find the optimal combination of indicator weights and parameters that maximize a custom reward function.
  • Why Random Forest?To act as a "risk filter". Even if a signal is generated, the ML model assesses the probability of profit. If the confidence is low, the trade is skipped.

We define a custom reward function to guide the swarm towards a balance of high profit, high win rate, and stability.
