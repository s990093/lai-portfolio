Abstract

I participated in the14th IEEE Global Conference on Consumer Electronics (GCCE 2025) to present my research paper. The presentation focused on the application of Multi-Entropy methods and the Mamba model in the field of network security.

Through theoretical analysis and experimental verification, I demonstrated the effectiveness of the proposed method in detecting abnormal traffic. During the conference, I also attended sessions by other scholars on the latest research in AI, communications, and consumer electronics, gaining valuable insights.

Research Presentation

This was my first time personally attending an international academic conference. My presentation, titled "An Efficient DDoS-Attack Detection System Based on Multi-Entropy Clustering and the Mamba Model," introduced a two-stage detection framework.

  • Stage 1: Multi-Entropy Features (Shannon, Rényi, Min Entropy) for traffic clustering with PSO-optimized GMM.
  • Stage 2: Mamba Structured State Space Model for deep sequence analysis.
  • Result: 5.5x inference speedup and 1/3 memory usage compared to Transformers.
